The Food Delivery process defines the following transitions:
The process starts from "customer order".
The process ends with "order completed".
From "customer order", the process continues with "is the customer registered?".
From "is the customer registered?", the process can continue with "create an account" if the branch is "no".
From "is the customer registered?", the process can continue with "recover client info" if the branch is "yes".
From "create an account", the process continues with "check credit card and the payment".
From "recover client info", the process continues with "check payment".
From "select rider and create work proposal", the process continues with "send work proposal".
From "Estimated Arrival Time", the process continues with "send to customer the waiting time".
From "send work proposal", the process continues with "has the order been accepted?".
From "notification order complete", the process continues with "receive Customer Satisfaction".
From "notification order retired", the process continues with "notification order retired to the client".
From "notification order retired to the client", the process continues with "notification order complete".
From "receive Customer Satisfaction", the process continues with "pay rider".
From "pay rider", the process continues with "order completed".
From "has the order been accepted?", the process can continue with "the rider refused the order" if the branch is "no".
From "has the order been accepted?", the process can continue with "the rider accepted the order" if the branch is "yes".
